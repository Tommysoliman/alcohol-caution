One U.S. standard drink contains approximately 14 grams of pure alcohol. Cocktail alcohol content varies considerably by recipe and pour size. Food can delay absorption, but its effect varies. BAC estimates cannot precisely predict behaviour or subjective feelings, and people can be impaired before they feel drunk.

The model must not be used to determine whether someone can drive. The only reliable way to know BAC is through appropriate testing.

Emergency warning: Anyone who is difficult to wake, vomiting repeatedly, breathing slowly or irregularly, confused, having seizures, or showing pale/blue skin may have alcohol poisoning. Contact emergency services immediately and do not leave them alone.
